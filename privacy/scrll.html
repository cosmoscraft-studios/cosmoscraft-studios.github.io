Overview

Scrll (“the App”) is designed with privacy in mind. We believe your personal memories should remain personal. This Privacy Policy explains how Scrll handles data and what information, if any, is collected when you use the App.

Data Collection

  • No personal data is collected. Scrll does not collect names, email addresses, account information, or contact details.
  • Photos and videos are accessed locally on your device only and are never uploaded, stored, or analyzed.
  • No location data, health data, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers are collected.

Purpose of Data Use

  • Displaying photos and videos stored locally on your device
  • Unlocking and restoring in-app purchase functionality
  • Responding to support requests when you contact us

In-App Purchases

Scrll offers a one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed exclusively by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment or billing information.

Third-Party Services

Scrll uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchase status and entitlement restoration. RevenueCat processes limited technical data such as an anonymous app user identifier and purchase status. This data is used solely for app functionality and is not used for advertising or tracking.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Analytics and Tracking

Scrll does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. No data is shared across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties. All photos and videos remain entirely on your device.

Children’s Privacy

Scrll does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected, the App is suitable for all age groups.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at support@cosmoscraftstudios.com.
